Research

I work on trustworthy AI agents and adversarial machine learning. My current research asks what it costs to defend an agent’s memory against poisoning, and how agents in multi-agent systems can trust the memory they share. I am also interested in human–AI interaction.

Current Research

2026 · Submitted, AGENT @ ICSE 2027

The Price of Safety

  • Measures what memory-poisoning defenses cost an LLM agent on attack-free traffic, on the LoCoMo benchmark.
  • Write-time defenses: no detectable utility cost.
  • Read-time reranker: −4.4 points accuracy; wrongly flags 9.9% of retrieved memories, removing memory from 45% of answers.

[PDF][arXiv]

With Hao Liu · To be submitted, AAAI 2027 workshop

Outcome-Bound Trust

  • Turns agent messages into checkable claims, and caps what an agent commits to each counterparty by the claims it has already honored.
  • In a supply-chain simulation: cut losses from lying agents by 98.1% at a 5.43% utility cost.
  • Safety invariants model-checked in TLA+.
